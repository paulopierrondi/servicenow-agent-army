**Hero**: Gallery case 08 (performance ACL chain) + Now Assist surface decision
**Metrica alvo**: saves + DM
**Slot na rotacao**: Now Assist

### Versao EN

Now Assist on top of a broken ACL chain is paid theater.

A 7-second list view. The team's first instinct: add Now Assist Case Summarization to mask the wait. Adding AI on top of slow ACL stacks credit cost on top of latency.

Performance Tuner ran read-only diagnostics. Five offenders: recursive ACL evaluation on account.industry, a chained Business Rule calling current.update inside another Business Rule, two unindexed custom fields used in list filters, and a GlideAggregate inside a Display Business Rule. SADA framed three options: fix the offenders, shard custom fields into a related table, or layer Now Assist Case Summarization on top. The council picked option A on cost and pure ROI. Option C refused as anti-pattern.

Target list-view p95 below 1.2 seconds after the fix, validated with before-after metrics on prod-equivalent sub-prod data. Zero Now Assist credits spent on this surface. Budget kept for surfaces that actually need AI.

Which surface in your instance has Now Assist as a workaround for a structural problem?

## Dia 3 — 2026-04-29 (quarta) — SADA Principio 1

**Hero**: SADA framework, Principio 1 ("Source of truth e a Now Platform, nao o prompt")
**Metrica alvo**: saves
**Slot na rotacao**: SADA

### Versao EN

Source of truth is the Now Platform, not the prompt.

Half the AI Agent Studio designs I review put the decision logic inside the prompt. The agent reads three KB excerpts the architect pasted in markdown. Two months later the KB changes. The prompt does not. The agent answers from a snapshot, not the source.

SADA Principle 1: every agent that decides reads from a table, KB, or metric source an auditor can trace. Prompt is instruction, not data. In the army, the Catalog Designer refuses a design where catalog content lives in the prompt body. It points to sc_cat_item. The CSM Specialist refuses Q&A on a static prompt; it requires Now Assist Q&A bound to a curated KB scope with cite-source on every answer.

Same outcome, lower credit cost long term, and a shape a BACEN auditor accepts in 24h.

Pull one agent design on your side. Where does its decision data live — in a table, or in the prompt?

github.com/paulopierrondi/servicenow-agent-army

### Versao PT-BR

Source of truth e a Now Platform, nao o prompt.

Metade dos designs de AI Agent Studio que revejo coloca a logica de decisao dentro do prompt. O agente le tres trechos de KB que o arquiteto colou em markdown. Dois meses depois a KB muda. O prompt nao. O agente responde de um snapshot, nao da fonte.

Principio 1 do SADA: todo agente que decide le de tabela, KB, ou indicator com trilha auditavel. Prompt e instrucao, nao dado. No batalhao, o Catalog Designer recusa design em que catalogo vive no corpo do prompt. Aponta pra sc_cat_item. O CSM Specialist recusa Q&A em prompt estatico; exige Now Assist Q&A com escopo de KB curada e cite-source em toda resposta.

Mesmo outcome, custo de credit menor no longo prazo, e formato que auditoria BACEN aceita em 24h.

Pega um design de agente do seu lado. Onde mora o dado que ele decide — tabela, ou prompt?

### Versao EN

CAB became theater: 90 normal changes per month approved in one hour, no real risk discussion.

The team wanted Now Assist Change Risk. Risk wanted the model explained, not "trust the box". Auto-approval on a BACEN-touching change was off the table from minute one.
